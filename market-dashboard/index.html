Indexed to 100 = (price ÷ price on the first day of the window) × 100. A line at 120 means the stock is up 20% over the window. This strips out share-price levels so a ₹3,000 stock and a ₹300 stock can be compared on one axis.

CompanyPrice ₹BVPS ₹P/BTTM EPS ₹P/E
P/B = daily close ÷ most recently reported quarterly book value per share. P/E = daily close ÷ trailing-twelve-month EPS, where TTM EPS is the last four quarters of reported PAT divided by shares outstanding. Both step up or down on the day a quarter closes, so a falling line means the price fell or the fundamentals improved.
